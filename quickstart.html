The 30-second mental model

tmux runs your terminal sessions: split panes side by side (nvim + Claude), and keep everything alive after you close iTerm2. Its prefix key is Ctrl+a — press & release it, then hit the next key.

nvim is modal: you're in Normal mode (moving/commands) by default; press i to Insert (type text), and Esc or jk to go back. Its leader key is Space.

Move between everything — nvim splits, tmux panes, the Claude terminal — with the same Ctrl+h j k l. No prefix, no leader.
